The first cut at this template used flexbox with `width: 140.5mm` on each card and it broke on long titles ("Jiu-Jitsu per Principianti -- Workshop Gratuito") -- flex children grow past their declared width to fit content, two cards overflowed the sheet width, the row wrapped, and we got 4 cards stacked vertically across 2 pages instead of 2x2 on one page. Absolute positioning is deterministic: each card sits in a fixed 140.5x97mm box, overflow is hidden inside, content can be any length and the layout cannot change.
